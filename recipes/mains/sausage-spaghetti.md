# Sausage Spaghetti

Garlicky tomato sauce with sausage, sized for one can of tomatoes.

## Ingredients

- Spaghetti (as much as you like)
- A decent amount of garlic-infused olive oil
- A shit ton of garlic (6–10 cloves)
- 1 can San Marzano tomatoes
- Salt
- Red pepper flakes
- A pinch of sugar
- Hot Italian sausage (already cooked)
- Plenty of salt for the pasta water

## Equipment

- Pan or pot with a lid
- Large pot for the pasta

## Steps

1. **Garlic oil:** Add a good amount of garlic-infused olive oil to the pan, then the garlic. Turn the burner to 2 and lightly toast the garlic for a couple of minutes. Squish the cloves with a spoon to get all the garlic goodness out.
2. **Sausage:** If your sausages are already cooked, set them aside now. They only need to warm through in the sauce at the end.
3. **Tomatoes:** Take the garlic out. Add the San Marzano tomatoes once the oil is hot enough that they sizzle a bit, then crush them with a spoon.
4. **Season:** Add salt, red pepper flakes, and a pinch of sugar.
5. **Reduce:** Cook about 10 minutes with the lid off so the sauce thickens.
6. **Simmer:** Turn the heat down to 1–2, put the lid on, and cook another 10 minutes or so.
7. **Pasta:** Salt the shit out of your pasta water and cook the spaghetti.
8. **Finish:** Cut up the sausage and add it to the sauce just to warm through.

## Notes

- Burner settings (2 for the garlic, 1–2 for the simmer) are dial numbers on the home stove.
- Open question: how to handle raw sausage (the voice note only covers already-cooked).
